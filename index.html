Who We Are at Anthoney's

In 1986, chicken farming in Sri Lanka meant one thing: volume. We started as a small family farm with a different idea — that healthy food and honest farming weren't a cost to be minimised, they were the whole point.

Today, New Anthoney's Farms (Pvt) Ltd raises chicken to international poultry standards, farm fresh and 100% natural — with a long-term commitment to environmental sustainability and the communities we work in.

Our Standard

We call it the Green Commandments

A set of standards we hold ourselves to that no law requires, covering everything from how our chicken is raised to how our farms treat the land. It's not a certificate we chase once and forget. It's the reason we're still the only ones who can claim it.
